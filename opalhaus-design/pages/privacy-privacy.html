Privacy Policy

At Opalhaus®, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard the data you share with us when you visit our website, contact us, or engage with our services.

Information We Collect

• Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number, company name, and any other details you voluntarily provide through contact forms or inquiries.

• Technical Information

IP address, browser type, device data, pages visited, and time spent on our website—collected via cookies and analytics tools.

• Project-Related Information

Any data you share with us during a project discussion or service engagement, including files, preferences, feedback, goals, and communication details, is securely protected.

How We Use Your Information

• Respond to inquiries or provide requested services

We use your information to respond effectively to your inquiries, deliver personalized services, and ensure smooth communication throughout the process.

• Improve our website, user experience, and service offerings

Collected data helps us enhance website functionality, understand user behavior, and optimize our services to better meet your evolving needs.

• Send important updates, news, or promotional content (only with your consent)

With your permission, we’ll share occasional updates, newsletters, or offers to keep you informed and engaged with our latest work.

• Maintain internal records and manage client relationships

We securely store your data to manage project history, improve service quality, and build lasting, efficient relationships with every client.

Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Our site uses secure encryption protocols and follows best practices to ensure data integrity.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and collect analytical data. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in our practices. We encourage you to review this page regularly. The most recent update date will always be noted at the top of the page for your reference, ensuring continued transparency and trust.
